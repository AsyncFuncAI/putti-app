# Putti

Your own helpers. Just talk.

Putti gives you a small team of AI helpers, each with its own chat. They work on their own cloud
computer, so they keep going when you close your Mac: planning, looking things up, writing, and
doing jobs on a schedule. Link the services you use once (Linear, Notion, GitHub and more) and every
helper can use them, and anything that changes something asks you first.

## Download

**[Download Putti for the Mac](https://github.com/AsyncFuncAI/putti-app/releases/latest/download/Putti_aarch64.dmg)**

Or start at [sharenow.today/putti](https://sharenow.today/putti).

- macOS 13 or later, on an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer)
- A Claude or ChatGPT plan for your helpers to think with
- Free for your first week; after that Putti needs a sharenow plan

Open the disk image, drag Putti to Applications, and sign in with Google or an emailed code. Putti
updates itself: **Putti › Check for Updates…** looks right away.
